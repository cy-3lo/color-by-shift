Each tile is a painting as the game would show it once recovered: five colours, flat blocks, a foam of cells that follows the outlines. Columns are colour choices, rows are numbers of cells. Click a tile to pick that painting with those colours and that many cells; click it again to drop it. ΔE is the smallest difference between two of the five colours (larger is easier to tell apart); groups is the number of edge groups of that foam.
