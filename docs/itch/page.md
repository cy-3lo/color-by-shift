Color by Shift

Drag one cell: every like pair shifts.

Drag a misplaced cell toward a neighbor: every pair of the same two colors shifts with it.
Solve the puzzle and the painting comes back.

---

Made for GitHub Game Off 2026 (theme: Shift). A game by Cy-3LO.
Source: https://github.com/cy-3lo/color-by-shift

Paintings: 15 public-domain works from The Cleveland Museum of Art, The Metropolitan Museum
of Art, the National Gallery of Art (Washington) and rawpixel (CC0). Icons: Ionicons (MIT).
Engine: Godot (MIT).

Use of AI: most of the code was written with Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code, directed,
played and reviewed by Cy-3LO. No image or sound is AI-generated.
